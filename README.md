# ShopSphere E-Commerce Analytics Dashboard

![Dashboard Banner](https://img.shields.io/badge/Status-Active-brightgreen) ![Power%20BI](https://img.shields.io/badge/Tool-PowerBI-blue) ![License](https://img.shields.io/badge/License-MIT-green)

## 📊 Project Overview

ShopSphere is a comprehensive **Power BI analytics dashboard** designed to provide actionable insights into e-commerce business performance. This project analyzes sales data across multiple product categories, sales channels, and geographic regions to drive data-informed decision-making.

The dashboard covers **12,000+ orders** spanning a full calendar year with detailed breakdowns by product category, sales channel, delivery performance, and profitability metrics.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

E-commerce businesses struggle with:
- **Fragmented visibility** into sales performance across multiple channels
- **Unclear profitability drivers** at product and category levels
- **Inconsistent delivery performance** impacting customer satisfaction
- **Discount impact analysis** - understanding which discounts drive profit vs. loss
- **Geographic performance gaps** - regional and city-level insights unavailable
- **Seasonal trend identification** - missed opportunities for strategic planning

---

## 🎓 Objectives

✅ **Centralize business metrics** - Create a single source of truth for all KPIs  
✅ **Identify top performers** - Analyze which categories, channels, and regions drive revenue  
✅ **Optimize profitability** - Track profit margins and discount impact analysis  
✅ **Monitor delivery efficiency** - Analyze on-time delivery rates by region and channel  
✅ **Enable data-driven decisions** - Provide interactive dashboards for stakeholders  
✅ **Support strategic planning** - Identify trends and seasonality patterns

## 🔍 Business Insights

### 1. **Category Performance**
- All three categories (Electronics, Fashion, Home & Furniture) demonstrate strong performance
- Electronics leads in variety with 3 sub-categories
- Relatively balanced profitability across categories

### 2. **Sales Channel Distribution**
- **Website** is the dominant sales channel with highest profitability
- **Mobile App** shows emerging potential as secondary channel
- **Marketplace** integration provides diversification

### 3. **Delivery Performance Opportunities**
- **Fast delivery (22.3%)** represents premium service tier
- **Delayed deliveries (10.92%)** indicate operational challenges
- Geographic variation suggests regional logistics improvements needed

### 4. **Discount Strategy Insights**
- Discount impact varies significantly by product and time period
- Need to evaluate discount ROI more carefully
- Some discounts drive losses; optimization required

### 5. **Profitability Metrics**
- **7.72% profit margin** is reasonable for e-commerce but has optimization potential
- Profit concentration by category requires strategic focus
- Sub-category performance shows varying margin profiles

### 6. **Seasonal Patterns**
- Monthly trends remain relatively stable throughout the year
- Limited seasonal volatility suggests consistent demand
- Opportunity to introduce seasonal strategies

## 💡 Recommendations

### Short-term Actions (0-3 months)
1. **Optimize Delivery Performance**
   - Investigate root causes of 10.92% delayed deliveries
   - Set target to reduce delays to <5%
   - Implement regional logistics improvements

2. **Discount Strategy Review**
   - Analyze profitability of discounted vs. non-discounted orders
   - Establish discount guidelines by product category
   - Eliminate unprofitable discount schemes

3. **Channel Performance Monitoring**
   - Establish weekly KPI tracking by sales channel
   - Identify why Mobile App underperforms
   - Develop channel-specific strategies

### Medium-term Initiatives (3-6 months)
1. **Category-level Optimization**
   - Conduct margin analysis on each sub-category
   - Identify high-margin product opportunities
   - Optimize inventory for top-performing SKUs

2. **Geographic Expansion**
   - Analyze regional profitability differences
   - Focus marketing spend on high-return regions
   - Improve delivery infrastructure in underperforming areas

3. **Customer Segmentation**
   - Develop customer segments based on purchase behavior
   - Create targeted marketing campaigns
   - Implement loyalty program for high-value customers

### Long-term Strategy (6+ months)
1. **Predictive Analytics**
   - Implement demand forecasting models
   - Enable proactive inventory management
   - Predict customer churn and retention drivers

2. **Profitability Improvement**
   - Target 9-10% profit margin through operational efficiency
   - Optimize cost structure across categories
   - Invest in high-margin product lines

3. **Digital Transformation**
   - Enhance mobile app user experience
   - Integrate marketplace data in real-time
   - Build predictive recommendation engine

## 📝 Notes & Assumptions

**Data Assumptions:**
- All monetary values are in millions (M) unless otherwise specified
- Date range covers a complete 12-month calendar year
- Profit margin calculated as: (Total Profit / Total Sales) × 100
- Delivery speed categories are pre-defined in source data
- Regional and city aggregations based on operational boundaries

**Project Scope:**
- This dashboard focuses on historical performance analysis
- Real-time operational metrics are not included
- Customer-level PII is excluded for privacy compliance
- Data reflects core e-commerce operations only
